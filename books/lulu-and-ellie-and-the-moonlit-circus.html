Lulu & Ellie Adventures · Book 4

Lulu & Ellie and the Moonlit Circus

A traveling circus glows beneath the moon, but its performers have lost the wonder that once made the whole show shine.

Lulu is ready to leap straight into the spotlight. Ellie notices that the real problem is quieter than a missing trick or broken prop.

By listening to the performers and following the circus's small clues, the sisters help the show remember why it began.

The adventure celebrates confidence, creativity, teamwork, and the courage to try again.

Inside the Story

Help the circus find its wonder again.
