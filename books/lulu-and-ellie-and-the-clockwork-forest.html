Lulu & Ellie and the Clockwork Forest

A ticking forest of brass leaves, hidden gears, and careful choices asks Lulu and Ellie to find the rhythm that keeps it alive.

Inside a forest of brass leaves, wooden gears, clockflowers, and soft chimes, the natural rhythm has begun to slip out of time.

Lulu wants to rush the mechanism. Ellie hears the pattern hidden inside it, and together they help the forest find its living rhythm again.

Young readers get a clear emotional shape: a problem to understand, a world worth helping, and a finish that feels kind.

Inside the Story

Listen for the pattern beneath the ticking.

The forest does not need to be forced back into order. It needs someone patient enough to hear what changed.

Brass-leaf paths, wooden gears, clockflower clearings, and a forest that keeps time by listening to every small sound.

The sisters learn that careful attention can restore a system more gently than rushing to repair it.
