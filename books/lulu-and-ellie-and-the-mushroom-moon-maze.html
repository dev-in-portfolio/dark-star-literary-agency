Lulu & Ellie Adventures · Book 6

Lulu & Ellie and the Mushroom Moon Maze

A moonlit maze of glowing mushrooms shifts until the sisters learn how to listen to the light.

Lulu tries to outrun the changing paths. Ellie notices that the mushroom glow is not random; it is answering their choices.

The maze becomes understandable when the sisters slow down, compare what they noticed, and follow the gentlest signal.

The story turns confusion into curiosity and shows that the right route is not always the fastest one.
