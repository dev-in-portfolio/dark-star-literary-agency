Lulu & Ellie Adventures · Book 7

Lulu & Ellie and the Lanterns of the Deep

An undersea lantern procession leads to a hidden world of songs, currents, and a mystery tucked below the waves.

Lulu follows the brightest lantern. Ellie notices that the procession is moving to a rhythm carried through the water.

The sisters must understand the songs and currents before they can help the lanterns reach their true destination.

The adventure turns the deep sea into a luminous, welcoming world rather than a frightening one.

Inside the Story

Follow the lanterns, then listen to the current.

Glowing sea paths, coral keys, quiet songs, and shifting currents reward careful observation.

What young readers will love

A bright underwater world, a lantern parade, friendly sea creatures, and a mystery solved through sound and movement.

What adults can expect

A gentle story about listening, cooperation, and understanding a different world's way of communicating.
