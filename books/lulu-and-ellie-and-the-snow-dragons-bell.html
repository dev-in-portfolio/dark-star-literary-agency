Lulu & Ellie Adventures · Book 5

Lulu & Ellie and the Snow Dragon's Bell

A snowy mountain kingdom, a shy snow dragon, and a bell that can wake the right kind of magic.

The kingdom expects a roar, but the young dragon is quiet, careful, and frightened of getting the bell's ancient song wrong.

Lulu brings courage in motion. Ellie notices that the bell responds to patience rather than noise.

Together they help the dragon discover that bravery does not have to sound the same for everyone.

Inside the Story

A quiet dragon can still wake powerful magic.

Snow-covered paths, frozen towers, soft bells, and a gentle dragon shape an adventure about courage without pressure.

What young readers will love

A snowy fantasy setting, a lovable dragon, a magical bell, and a problem solved through kindness.

What adults can expect

A reassuring story about confidence, patience, and respecting different ways of being brave.
