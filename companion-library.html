How this preview works

Source existence, readiness, and availability are tracked separately.

Every entry is tied to an exact supplied PDF through a checksum-backed source manifest. Readiness labels describe remaining review work; they are not release dates.

Developed preview

A strong preview that still needs final editorial, accessibility, and production checks.

Final QA pending

A developed interior awaiting a complete final quality-assurance pass.

Revision pass pending

A developed interior that needs focused layout or character revision.
