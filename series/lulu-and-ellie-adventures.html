Lulu & Ellie Adventures

Magical portal adventures where two scruffy terrier sisters help strange worlds out of trouble through courage, careful noticing, and teamwork.

Books 1-10

Clockwork Forest is Book 3, followed by Moonlit Circus, Snow Dragon's Bell, Mushroom Moon Maze, Lanterns of the Deep, the Book That Lost Its Ending, the Island That Drifted Away, and the Star Map of Everywhere.

What Readers Will Find

Warm adventures with clues, charms, maps, helpers, and return visits.

Portal worlds

Every adventure opens into a distinct magical place with its own rules and needs.

Two kinds of brave

Lulu's bold leaps and Ellie's careful noticing solve problems better together.

Gentle stakes

The worlds need understanding, patience, and help more than conquering.

Story continuity

Recurring charms, clues, and emotional rhythms reward readers who return.
