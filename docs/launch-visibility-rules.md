# Launch Visibility Rules

This document describes how future content should be handled on the public site.

## Publicly Visible

Public navigation may include:

- Live books and collections with evidence-based marketplace information
- Recorded official marketplace links that clearly require visitors to confirm current price, format, and availability
- Available learning lines
- Real downloads
- Official brand pages
- Preorder pages with working official preorder links
- Intentional preview pages that are clearly labeled `Coming Soon`, `In the Works`, or `Preview`

## Intentional Preview Requirements

A preview page is a deliberate public announcement, not an unfinished internal page. It must:

- Use a visible preview status near the top of the page
- Avoid claiming that the book, collection, or learning line is currently available
- Avoid publishing an unconfirmed release date
- Avoid active-looking purchase controls when no purchase link exists
- Use approved public titles and descriptions only
- Link back to a live library, series, or contact page

A preview may show approved cover art, animation, or interior feature art when those assets are intentionally public.

## Keep Hidden Until Ready

Keep the following out of public navigation unless they meet the intentional-preview requirements:

- Internal series or collection drafts
- Unapproved titles
- Unapproved book pages
- Free-download pages whose files are not ready
- Rights or licensing pages that have not intentionally launched
- Press kit pages that have not intentionally launched
- School or library pages that have not intentionally launched
- Brand guidelines
- Production notes, codes, and internal terminology

## Marketplace Accuracy

- Marketplace buttons must point to recorded official product destinations.
- A recorded ASIN or product URL is not treated as proof of current price, format, stock, or orderability.
- Until a manual marketplace review is recorded, use language such as `Amazon link on file`, `Current status unverified`, and `Check Amazon`.
- Do not hard-code marketplace prices unless the repository records the verification date and the site has a defined refresh process.
- When no current official product link is on file, show a non-interactive status rather than a placeholder button.
- Preview pages must not imply availability.
- Prefer clear non-interactive status text over `href="#"` purchase placeholders.

## Original Adventure Preview Entries

The Original Adventure media archive may retain numbered preview entries before full public book pages are ready. Those entries must remain clearly preview-only until their approved titles, descriptions, and marketplace status are published.

## Release Rule

Future content may be built internally ahead of time, but it should not be added to public navigation until it is ready for purchase, preorder, sample download, or an intentional and clearly labeled teaser.
